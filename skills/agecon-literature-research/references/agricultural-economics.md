# Agricultural economics routing

For agricultural economics and development economics, expand each topic through
economic decisions and institutional conditions, not keyword similarity alone.

## Concept lanes

- Actors: smallholders, family farms, cooperatives, service providers, machinery
  owners, village collectives, local governments, input/output firms.
- Decisions: adoption, outsourcing, entry, acreage, input use, labor allocation,
  investment, participation, compliance, crop choice, exit.
- Institutions: land tenure, service contracts, subsidies, credit, collective
  governance, market access, agricultural extension, disaster/risk policy.
- Mechanisms: transaction costs, fixed-cost sharing, search/matching, learning,
  liquidity, risk, labor substitution, scale economies, competition, bargaining.
- Outcomes: yield, total factor productivity, cost, profit, technical efficiency,
  allocative efficiency, food security, environment, inequality, resilience.
- Data: household surveys, village panels, firm registration, service-provider
  surveys, fixed observation points, administrative subsidy records, remote
  sensing, weather, soil, prices, machinery trajectories.

## Method reporting

Separate descriptive association from causal identification. For causal claims,
record treatment timing, comparison group, identifying assumption, fixed effects,
standard-error level, and major threats. Do not equate a named estimator with a
credible design.

For DID, flag staggered adoption and heterogeneous treatment effects. For IV,
record first-stage relevance and the exclusion restriction. For mechanism
claims, do not treat the Baron-Kenny three-step sequence as causal mechanism
identification.

## Synthesis priorities

- Unit and level of analysis.
- Spatial and temporal scope.
- Directly observed versus constructed variables.
- Whether “service availability”, “service use”, “market competition”, and
  “service scale” are conceptually distinct.
- Policy eligibility/endogeneity and implementation heterogeneity.
- External-validity limits across crops, regions, farm sizes, and market stages.
